and source code. The asset groups below have separate terms.

the fonts. See `assets/fonts/manifest.json` for exact download URLs and SHA-256
checksums.

## Generated project imagery

The README hero in `docs/assets/` and the direction studies in `assets/examples/`
were generated for this project. They are published to illustrate the skill's
art direction. The software's MIT license does not grant a separate license to
redistribute these images as standalone artwork or templates. Contact the
maintainer for reuse beyond displaying the project documentation.

The README hero's generation prompt is preserved in
`docs/readme-cover-prompt.txt`. The images demonstrate a visual language; do not
present them as evidence of exact font-file typesetting or completed exports
for every target platform.
